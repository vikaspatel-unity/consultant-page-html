FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you build full games or embed as a Senior Unity Engineer?

Both. I can build full titles and vertical slices from greenfield to release, develop core gameplay systems (combat, character controllers, physics, multiplayer), or embed directly with your engineering team as a Senior Unity Engineer to accelerate your milestone velocity.

What does a Unity Code Review & Architecture Audit include?

I perform a deep, comprehensive audit of your Unity codebase. You receive an actionable report covering architectural anti-patterns, memory leak locations, garbage collection spikes, tight coupling risks, and a prioritized refactoring roadmap along with PR review standards for your team.

How do you help with AdMob consultancy and policy compliance?

I audit your existing ad waterfall, configure real-time bidding mediation (AppLovin, IronSource, Unity), optimize placement frequencies to maximize eCPM/ARPDAU, and implement Google UMP consent to resolve ad limits and policy warnings.

Can you resolve Apple App Store rejections?

Yes. I frequently resolve tough rejections including Guideline 4.3 (Design/Spam), 2.1 (App Completeness), and 5.1.1 (Privacy). I provide code refactoring, Apple Privacy Manifests (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), and professional appeal documentation.

How do you handle Google Play’s 20-tester / 14-day closed testing rule?

I help you structure, recruit, and manage the closed testing track with active testers, monitor telemetry, verify compliance feedback, and prepare the submission questionnaire so Google unlocks production access on the first review.

What does your Organic Growth (ASO) service include?

I conduct in-depth keyword analysis, optimize your store title, subtitle, and descriptions for both stores, design high-converting screenshot narratives, run A/B listing experiments, and implement smart in-app review prompts.

How do you optimize Unity games to achieve a stable 60 FPS?

I profile code using the Unity Profiler and Memory Profiler to eliminate Garbage Collection spikes, optimize draw calls via GPU instancing and SRP batcher, tune physics simulation steps, and compress textures to guarantee smooth 60 FPS performance on iOS & Android.

Do you work under NDA and maintain confidentiality?

Absolutely. NDAs and secure access protocols are standard practice. I work directly in your private repositories with least-privilege access and maintain complete confidentiality.
